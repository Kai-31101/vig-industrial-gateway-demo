# VSIP operating status audit — 12 November 2026

Scope: all 22 industrial park profiles in the published catalogue. Classification is an editorial interpretation of official developer, tenant, public institution or government/public broadcaster evidence, not a certification of every phase or current readiness of each plot.

## Classification rules

- Operational: evidence of established manufacturing or an explicitly operating park. This can be partial operation; the profile note preserves the phase limitation.
- Developing: confirmed groundbreaking or ongoing site/infrastructure works. A scheduled production date does not establish actual operation.
- Planned: approval/preparatory procedures established, but no actual groundbreaking confirmation found in this review.
- Unknown remains the default for a project with insufficient evidence; all 22 current projects have a supported milestone.
- Retrieval date (2026-11-12) is separate from the source/event date. An empty source date means the exact publication date was not established; it is never filled with retrieval date.
- Establishment, legal entity registration, occupancy, investment approval, and planned factory openings alone do not establish operating production.

## Findings and limitations

10 operational, 9 developing, 3 planned. Sources span different dates and the badges reflect the latest supported milestone found, not a real-time inspection. The operating label does not assert completion of every parcel or expansion phase.

The InvestVietnam directory labels Nghe An II and Lang Son with early operation dates that conflict with construction reporting. Those dates were not used as operational evidence. Bac Ninh II has planned factory commissioning dates but no actual manufacturing confirmation found; it remains developing. Nghe An III has planned groundbreaking dates but no actual groundbreaking confirmation found; its supported milestone remains preparatory procedures. Hai Phong II and Nghe An IV are based on the developer’s 29 May 2026 approval announcement, without inferring operation. Existing catalogue areas are unchanged because phase areas in approval announcements differ from total portfolio areas.

Can Tho is partially operational based on the Government newspaper’s 19 September 2026 report of Bestway phase 1 operation. Binh Duong III is operational based on the LEGO factory inauguration, with a phase-completion limitation. Existing Quang Ngai I activity is carefully separated from the new Quang Ngai II project in the same source family.

## Implementation and verification

Structured data: `src/vsipStatuses.json`; binding: `src/vsipStatuses.ts`. Every classification binds to a public source document. Profile overview shows rationale, source milestone date and retrieval date. Card/detail badge colors distinguish operation from development. Existing completeness ordering automatically incorporates the newly sourced status coverage.

Regression checks cover all catalogue slugs, document provenance, separate dates, phased operation, and separation of numbered projects. Production verification is recorded after deployment.
