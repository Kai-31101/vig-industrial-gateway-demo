# VSIP infrastructure, industry and contact enrichment

Research date: 12 November 2026. Coverage: 22 industrial park profiles.

## Source and interpretation rules

- All 22 infrastructure sets come from the individual VSIP project pages. Values are published specifications, not certified operating capacity, remaining tenant capacity or independent utility audits. No effective date is supplied by these pages; retrieval date is recorded separately.
- Power units are preserved: kV is voltage, MW is active power and MVA is apparent power. They are not interconverted. VSIP II Binh Duong and its expansion retain separate water/wastewater capacities.
- Airport/port distances are source-published distances, not computed travel routes. Gateway operational status remains unknown; proximity does not prove completion.
- Sources give explicit target sectors for some parks and existing investor sectors for others. The UI distinguishes these bases. They are not legal admission lists. Thai Binh remains pending confirmation because no sufficiently specific project sector list was found in the reviewed sources.
- The English source's ambiguous “argon-processing” for Lang Son is excluded rather than guessing that it means agricultural processing.
- VSIP contact directory and project-specific contact sections take priority. SingCham's VSIP sponsor listing fills Thai Binh, Quang Tri, Ha Tinh, Lang Son and Nam Dinh gaps. It is labeled as the directory source. Phone and email reachability have not been tested by calling or sending messages.
- Shared area offices are explicitly labeled. Quang Ninh uses the published group investment contact, not a fabricated local office. Office addresses are retained as published and are not substituted for project location addresses.
- Conflicts: the SingCham listing has different Quang Ngai/Binh Dinh contacts; the VSIP directory takes precedence. Other Sembcorp publications give different historical distances/areas for Hai Phong and Quang Ngai; this update uses each current VSIP project page for infrastructure and does not overwrite existing area figures. QTIP's government phase-1 works report gives different utility figures from the full-project VSIP page; displayed figures are explicitly published project specifications, not completed phase-1 capacity.
- Binh Duong I/II industry scope uses the EDB/Sembcorp 2022 sector infographic; Quang Ngai II uses Sembcorp's 2024 announcement. These dates appear in source attribution. All other sector records use project pages, Sembcorp's Hai Phong page, Invest Vietnam or the VSIP QTIP announcement.
